Project Theme

Seaglass Sanctuary is a mental health awareness website designed to provide accessible information, supportive resources, and a calm digital space for individuals seeking understanding, encouragement, and connection.

Purpose of the Website

This project was created for a Business Professionals of America competitive event. The goal of the site is to raise awareness about mental health topics, reduce stigma, and promote emotional well-being through educational content and curated resources.

Seaglass Sanctuary represents resilience — shaped by challenges, smoothed by time, and still beautiful in every form.
